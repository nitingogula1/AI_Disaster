# Disaster Command Dashboard & Active Theaters

## 1. Dashboard Overview
The Disaster Command Dashboard (`/command`) serves as the chief operational interface for incident commanders. It consolidates KPI metrics, interactive GIS flood maps, drone HUD feeds, and monitored village directories into a single unified screen.

---

## 2. Active Disaster Theaters

SentinelAid features pre-modeled, authentic disaster operational theaters:

### 2.1 Theater 1: Nuwakot & Rasuwa (Trishuli River Basin, Nepal)
* **Hazard Type**: Extreme Himalayan Monsoon Flash Flood, River Confluence Swelling & Debris Flow.
* **Primary Waterway**: Trishuli River, Langtang Khola, Tadi River.
* **Monitored Locations**:
  * **Rasuwagadhi** (Border Post, 2.4m flood, cut off)
  * **Timure** (Gorge river surge, 2.1m flood)
  * **Syabrubesi** (Confluence overflow, 1.8m flood)
  * **Dhunche** (District Headquarters, command staging)
  * **Ramche** (Mudslide & road fissure, 1.4m depth)
  * **Betrawati** (Bridge approach washed out, 1.6m depth)
  * **Trishuli Bazaar / Bidur** (DEOC tactical command base, 1.85m flood)
  * **Battar** (Elevated plateau safe haven & district hospital, 0.0m flood)
  * **Devighat** (Downstream confluence, 0.85m flood)
  * **Dhading Besi** (Precautionary monitoring, 0.5m flood)

### 2.2 Theater 2: Kathmandu Urban Basin (Bagmati River, Nepal)
* **Hazard Type**: Urban Flash Inundation & Drainage Backflow.
* **Monitored Wards**: Balkhu, Teku Confluence, Thapathali, Sankhamul, Koteshwor Corridor.

### 2.3 Theater 3: Assam Valley (Brahmaputra River, India)
* **Hazard Type**: Major River Embankment Breach.
* **Monitored Zones**: Guwahati Riverfront, Kamrup Central School, Kamakhya Hill Haven, Dispur HQ.

### 2.4 Theater 4: Rajam Urban Catchment (Andhra Pradesh, India)
* **Hazard Type**: Cyclonic Storm Runoff & Lowland Canal Breach (Tactical Preparedness Scenario).
* **Monitored Points**: GMRIT Campus (Elevated Haven), Boddam, Cherugudupeta, Prasanthi Nagar, Sitampeta Ridge.

## 3. Operational Data Mode Toggle
Located at the top of the Monitored Places Directory table:
* **🌊 Flood Drill Mode**: Shows simulated water submergence depths, trapped casualties, and road hazards for evacuation planning.
* **☀️ Normal Clear Mode**: Shows calm baseline conditions ($0.0	ext{m}$ water, $0$ casualties, $100\%$ open roads).
